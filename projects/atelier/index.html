OUR SARTORIAL PHILOSOPHY

MADE WITH INTENTION.

At ATELIER, a suit is never a commodity. It is a harmonious dialogue between the drape of natural cloth and the natural anatomy of the wearer.

Every bespoke garment begins with a unique paper pattern cut by hand for your posture, shoulder slope, and natural movement. Our master tailors assemble floating horsehair chest canvasses that mold to your torso over time, yielding unmatched comfort and effortless drape that synthetic fused garments can never achieve.

II.

FOUR STAGES OF BESPOKE

Our Process

From initial sketch to your final fitting, the bespoke journey is an intimate, creative collaboration.

01

Consultation

Over coffee or tea in our Mayfair rooms, we discuss your lifestyle, garment purpose, and aesthetic preferences while reviewing swatches from our curated cloth archives.

02

Measurements

We record over 32 precise anatomical measurements, noting shoulder slope, posture cadence, and chest balance to draft your one-of-a-kind individual paper pattern.

03

Craftsmanship

Cloth is hand-sheared and basted into a skeleton garment. During your intermediate fittings, we sculpt lapel roll, balance, and sleeve pitch with millimeter precision.

04

Final Fitting

Your completed garment is pressed by hand using heavy vintage irons. You try on your finished tailoring for a final review before delivery in our monogrammed garment bag.
